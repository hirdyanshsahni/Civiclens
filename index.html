TRANSPARENT TRIAGE

How CIVICLENS ImpactScore works

Each report receives a 0–100 score to help staff sort the queue. It is a prioritization aid, not a prediction of harm or a promise about response time.

Reported severity levelUp to 30Low 6 · Medium 14 · High 22 · Critical 30. City staff can correct it during review.
Potential safety impactUp to 30Category baseline; a small safety-keyword adjustment can add up to 5.
Community corroborationUp to 204 points per confirmation and 5 points per linked duplicate.
Report ageUp to 202 points per day, capped at 20.
Administrators may override a recommendation with a recorded reason. Every report shows its factor breakdown and any override.
